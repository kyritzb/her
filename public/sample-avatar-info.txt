To get started, you need a VRM avatar file.

Place your VRM file at: public/avatar.vrm

Where to get VRM avatars:

1. VRoid Studio (Free)
   - Download: https://vroid.com/studio
   - Create custom avatars and export as VRM

2. Ready Player Me (Free)
   - Website: https://readyplayer.me
   - Generate avatars, then convert to VRM format

3. Booth.pm (Paid)
   - Website: https://booth.pm
   - Search for "VRM" models

4. VRChat (If you have permission)
   - Export your existing VRChat avatars

Sample VRM files for testing:
- VRM Sample Models: https://github.com/vrm-c/vrm-specification/tree/master/samples

Once you have a VRM file:
1. Rename it to "avatar.vrm"
2. Place it in the public/ folder
3. Restart the development server
4. Your avatar should load automatically!
